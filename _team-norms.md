# Team norms

Placeholder. The real file is `apprentices/team-norms.md`, which is private and not tracked here.
If it doesn't exist yet, create it from this shape.

Cross-apprentice habits I set and I own the fix for.

**Why this file exists.** These would otherwise sit in the `apprentices/*-checkins.md` files as
normal follow-ups, next to whichever person they applied to. Same problem the escalations had:
one norm hitting three people reads as systemic here and as three unrelated coaching notes there.

**What belongs here:** a habit that spans more than one apprentice and that I can fix myself, by
stating a norm or changing a process I control.

**What doesn't, 1: anything owned outside this desk.** Curriculum, the CTD eval form, program-side
process. That's `apprentices/escalations.md`.

**What doesn't, 2: per-person evidence.** That stays in `apprentices/<name>.md`. A norm cites
apprentices as instances, it isn't a record of their performance.

**Never inflate the instance count.** Only apprentices where it was confirmed are instances.
Someone the question was never put to is `Unconfirmed`.

**A single-person habit is not a norm.** That's coaching, and it stays a normal follow-up next to
that person.

**`Norm` lines are name-free on purpose.** They lift straight out when the norm gets stated to the
team.
